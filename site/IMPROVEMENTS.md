# Landing-page revision

The page targets Mac users and developers arriving from GitHub and communities.
The primary action is copying the Homebrew command, followed by opening Defi and
granting Accessibility access. No analytics or conversion baseline was available
for this revision. The expected effects below are hypotheses.

## Changes and reasons

| Change | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Preserve the exact headline and approved Horizon backdrop | Keep the visual identity chosen during prior iterations. |
| Name the product category in the hero subheading | A new visitor can identify a macOS scrolling window manager before watching the clip. |
| Use task-oriented feature titles | Connect scrolling columns, workspaces, Overview, and widths to actions the visitor can try. |
| Show native macOS, alpha status, and MIT licensing | Give relevant project facts without invented endorsements or usage numbers. |
| Keep first-launch steps in the final installation section | Explain the steps after Brew while keeping the hero and navigation compact. |
| End the page with a copy-command button after the FAQ | Offer the installation action after compatibility and permission questions. |

The hero and feature recordings are real footage of Defi; the hero's composed
parts are disclosed in [MEDIA.md](MEDIA.md).

## Alternate copy

The implemented headline remains "Your desktop, without the edges." These are
alternatives for a future comparison, rather than additional selectors on the page.

| Headline | What it emphasizes |
| --- | --- |
| Your windows. One continuous workspace. | The spatial model. |
| Move through your Mac at the speed of a keypress. | Keyboard navigation. This needs footage that demonstrates responsiveness. |
| More windows. Less rearranging. | The benefit of automatic layout. |

Keep the compact command as the primary action for this audience. If visitors
miss its copy icon, test a visible "Copy install command" label within the same
control. First-launch instructions remain in the installation section. "View on GitHub" is an
alternative to the current shorter "GitHub" label.

## What to evaluate next

1. Compare the current category-first subheading with "Keep your windows in one
   scrolling strip. Switch tasks with your keyboard." Ask new visitors what Defi
   does and what they would do next.
2. Compare the existing terminal-heavy hero clip with a genuine editor, browser,
   and notes workflow. Keep framing and playback controls consistent.
3. Check whether first-launch links help visitors reach the app and understand
   Accessibility. A successful copy is an intent signal, not proof of installation.

Choose one comparison at a time once traffic and a measurement method are
available. Record the hypothesis and primary outcome before running it. This
revision adds no tracking and makes no claim of measured conversion improvement.
