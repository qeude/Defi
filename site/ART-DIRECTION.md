## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a custom cinematic abstract 3D artwork for a premium macOS application landing page called Defi, based on the idea 'without the edges'. Landscape wide 3:2 composition. NO TEXT, NO UI, NO logos. A single impossible horizon of luminous nacre: a vast perfectly smooth dark translucent curved surface, its exquisitely fine pearl-white rim arcs diagonally from the lower left edge toward the upper right, opening into deep black space. Sophisticated physically rendered silver/pearl/muted lavender refractions and large soft ambient violet light that feels reflected through glass, brightest at right-center, broad and diffuse below the horizon. Asymmetric, minimal, sculptural, tactile, deeply elegant art direction akin to a high-end Apple product campaign, with nuanced material depth and precise highlights. Surface should fill the bottom and right portions while the upper left and center remain near OLED black for overlay text and video. No striped ribbons, no thin fiber texture, no hard beams, no neon, no particles or stars, no planets, no spheres, no three floating glass panels. ONE monumental continuous surface with a luminous rim. Seamless deep black edges fading smoothly into #050505 on all sides, especially top and bottom. Polished photographic 3D material, generous negative space, luminous but restrained contrast. The final asset will sit behind a real screen recording and flow behind the next page section.
